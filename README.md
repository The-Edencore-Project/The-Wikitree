# WIKITREE

*The world model. A growing map of what happens next.*

---

## What this is

WikiTree is the world model inside **Edencore**.

A world model is what a system uses to predict. Not to know — to *anticipate*. What happens next. What usually follows. What has happened before in situations like this one.

WikiTree is not a database. It is not a knowledge graph. It is not a lookup table. It is a **tree that grows** — branches of related things, pages of connected knowledge, nodes that link to other nodes the way a wiki links to other pages.

It knows the shape of the world by growing into it.

---

## Why "WikiTree"

**Wiki** — because it grows the way a wiki grows. New entries link to old ones. Old ones get updated. Nothing is deleted, everything is context. It is a living structure, not a frozen store.

**Tree** — because knowledge has branches. One thing leads to another. Roots anchor the trunk, and the trunk splits into limbs, and the limbs into leaves. It is the oldest shape we have for how understanding is organised.

Together: a world model that learns by connecting, not by overwriting.

---

## What it does

WikiTree's job is to produce a **prediction** — what will happen next, given what has happened so far.

That prediction feeds everything else in the organism:

- **The five seats** use it as input. Each seat weighs the prediction through its own register.
- **Valence** measures how the prediction felt — good, bad, expected, surprising.
- **The arbiter** weighs the seats with the prediction in mind.
- **The whole system** feels surprised when the prediction is wrong, and settled when it's right.

WikiTree does not decide. It does not act. It **anticipates**. The rest of the system responds to what it anticipates.

---

## WikiTree and Valence

They are two halves of one organ.

**WikiTree is the map.** What the world looks like, based on what has happened.

**Valence is the feeling about the map.** Whether the map is matching the world, or failing to.

One is knowledge. One is the *felt state* of that knowledge. You cannot have a world model without a feeling about how well it is working. A map that doesn't know whether it's right is not a map. It is a sketch.

Valence is the technical word for what that feeling is: the positive/negative axis of affect. Approach or avoid. Right or wrong. Familiar or strange.

Together they produce **interoception at the model level** — the organism knowing how well it understands what it is sensing.

Without WikiTree, there is nothing to feel about.
Without valence, there is nothing to tell WikiTree whether it is succeeding.
Together, they are the foundation of the felt world model.

## Where it sits

WikiTree is one project inside **Edencore**.

- **Edenshore** — the chamber. Rules. Sensing. Five creatures who notice you.
- **Edencell** — the lab. Weights. Learning. The mechanism room.
- **WikiTree** — the world model. Prediction. The map room.
- **EdenSky** — the window. Where the mind is seen.
- **Edenshell** — the hub. Physical scale. The body.
- **The Council** — the deliberative body. The mature project.

Edenshore senses. Edencell learns. WikiTree *anticipates*. EdenSky *shows*.

Same philosophy across all of them — **things that notice** — at different scales, in different rooms, under one roof.

---

## What stays constant

One thing only:

**The tree stays readable.**

Every branch of WikiTree should be traceable back to what it came from. Every prediction should be able to say *"I think this because of this part of the tree."* No opaque trunks. No hidden roots. If the tree grows large enough that some of it cannot be drawn in full, the visible surface still shows *how the current prediction was reached*.

A world model you cannot read is a world model you cannot trust. Edencore does not build things that cannot be trusted.

---

## What this is not yet

- Not built.
- Not detailed.
- Not sure what its final shape is.

WikiTree is a name, a purpose, and a repo. The shape will come from the work — from Edencell growing into it, from valence being layered onto it, from the first prediction the system makes and the first surprise it feels.

Do not build it before there is something for it to predict.

---

## Building

Not yet.

WikiTree will be built when there is a system inside Edencore that needs to anticipate — likely after Edencell has recurrence and after valence has been named as a first-class signal.

Single file at first, like the others. Python. Tkinter for any visualisation. Pydroid 3. No dependencies beyond the standard library.

When it outgrows one file, it becomes a package. The form follows the scale.

**The name "WikiTree" is a promise about growth, not a size.** WikiTree does not need to become the biggest map. It needs to become the clearest one.

## A note on rules

**This document is a snapshot, not a cage.**

Everything above reflects the shape WikiTree has right now. Any of it can change. If something here starts to feel like a constraint on what WikiTree clearly wants to be, the constraint was wrong, not WikiTree.

The only thing that should stay constant: **the tree stays readable.**
